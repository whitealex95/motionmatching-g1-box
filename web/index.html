The box driver's databases

The databases of the box driver (sonic_g1_box/mm_driver.py), the data of lmm-g1-box's default variant. The first part shows the controller now: its state, the clip the robot plays and how the last searches matched. Below it is lmm-g1-box's database page. Each data variant has an Animation Database, the frames of its clips, and two Matching Databases that Motion Matching searches: Locomotion (walking empty-handed or carrying) and Box (picks and places). A Matching Database is made of ranges, runs of consecutive frames of one clip, and the search can start playing at any frame of a range. This page plays one range, or one whole clip, at a time, exactly as stored, with the root moved by the clip's own root motion from the origin.

The clip the robot plays

The playhead is the frame the robot plays now. The graph follows the clip playing, at most twice a second. Gaps are frames in no database, a paler part of the Box lane is a part the search does not jump into, and the last step lane runs from the last step before a grasp or a release to the first step after it.

Matching

FeatureWeightCost share

Each search finds the frame whose features are nearest the query. The cost share is the part of the squared cost of the last match that comes from each feature group. The dot marks the query in use. The controller takes the box controller when the cheapest side costs less than "enter", and goes back to Locomotion above "leave".

The clip of this motion

The outlined span is the motion that plays. Click the graph to jump there: outside the motion this plays the whole clip. Gaps in the Locomotion and Box lanes are frames in no Matching Database. A paler part of the Box lane is the end of a walk, from 0.5 s before its last step, or the middle of a SceneBot pick or place, before its grasp or release, which the search of the demo does not jump into, though a clip already playing goes on through it.
